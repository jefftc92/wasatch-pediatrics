Adult Aligners

Straighten your teeth without anyone noticing. Clear aligners are a discreet, comfortable, and removable way for adults to get the smile they've always wanted, on a schedule that fits real life.

Made for adult life

Nearly invisible at meetings, dinners, and in every photo.

Removable and easy

Take them out to eat and brush; there are no wires or brackets.

Honest guidance

Dr. Wendy Chu will tell you plainly if aligners or braces fit your smile best.

Why Adults Love Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are nearly invisible and removable, so they fit easily into a professional, social life. You take them out to eat and brush, and there are no wires or brackets to work around.

They're one of the most popular adult options we offer, and a common choice for fixing drift after orthodontics from years ago.

What Treatment Is Like

Low-drama and predictable, from the first scan to the finished smile.

  1. Scan & plan Dr. Chu maps your smile and designs your custom series of trays.
  2. Wear each set Most of the day and night, switching sets on schedule.
  3. Occasional check-ins Quick visits track progress and hand off your next trays.
  4. Retain the result A retainer keeps your new smile in place for good.

Why Straighten as an Adult?

Straighter teeth are easier to clean, which means healthier gums and fewer cavities over the years, and a balanced bite wears more evenly and eases jaw strain. Add the confidence of a smile you're glad to share, and the benefits reach well past appearance.

Who They're For, and When Braces Win

Aligners handle many common adult concerns like crowding, gaps, and relapse after past orthodontics. For more complex bites, Dr. Wendy Chu may suggest clear braces, which stay on around the clock and manage bigger corrections. A consultation is the best way to know which gets you the best result.

A better smile shouldn't have to interrupt your life. With aligners, most people never notice it happening.

Common questions

How discreet are clear aligners really?

Very. Most people will not notice you are wearing them, which is why they are so popular with working adults.

Do aligners work as well as braces?

For many cases, yes. For complex corrections, braces may be more efficient. Dr. Chu will give you an honest recommendation.

What do adult aligners cost?

Cost depends on how much movement is needed. We offer orthodontic financing and will explain your options clearly at a complimentary consultation.

Need to be seen?

Call your office first — most orthodontics are seen the same day.
