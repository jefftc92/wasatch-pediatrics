Sedation & Comfort

If the dentist makes your child anxious, safe sedation keeps them calm and comfortable, from light laughing gas to fully asleep care. We'll always start with the gentlest option that fits.

Always the lightest option

We recommend the gentlest sedation that still lets your child get great care.

Safe and monitored

Every plan starts with a health review, careful dosing, and monitoring throughout.

Calm, positive visits

Great for nervous kids, a strong gag reflex, or a lot of treatment in one visit.

What is sedation dentistry?

Sedation is simply a safe way to help your child relax during dental care. Some children feel nervous in the chair, have a strong gag reflex, or need a lot of treatment at once, and sedation lets us provide that care gently, so the visit stays positive.

It ranges from light laughing gas that wears off in minutes to fully asleep care for bigger treatment. We'll walk you through every option and answer your questions before you decide.

Who is sedation for, and when?

Sedation can help children who feel very nervous, have a strong gag reflex, have trouble sitting still, or need several treatments finished in one visit. It's also a real help for many children with special healthcare needs.

We recommend it when it genuinely makes care easier and more comfortable, never as a default. If lighter reassurance and a gentle pace are enough, that's what we'll do.

How we keep sedation safe

Every sedation plan starts with a careful review of your child's health history. We tailor the type and dose to their age and weight, deeper sedation is delivered by a trained anesthesia provider, and your child is monitored closely from start to finish.

We'll send clear instructions before the visit and go over recovery afterward, so you always know exactly what to expect.

We'll always recommend the lightest, safest option that lets your child get the care they need, comfortably.

Common questions

Is sedation dentistry safe for children?

Yes, when it's carefully dosed and monitored. We review your child's full health history first, tailor everything to their age and weight, and watch them closely throughout. We always recommend the lightest option that will do the job.

Will my child be asleep?

It depends on the option. Laughing gas and oral sedation keep your child awake and relaxed, while sleep dentistry and general anesthesia let them doze or sleep fully. We tailor the choice to your child.

How do you decide which sedation to use?

We consider your child's age, anxiety level, health history, gag reflex, and how much treatment is needed, then recommend the lightest, safest option that works.

Can my child eat before sedation?

For lighter options a small meal is usually fine, but deeper sedation needs a fasting window. We'll give you clear, specific instructions when we schedule the visit.

Need to be seen?

Call your office first — most pediatric dentistry are seen the same day.
