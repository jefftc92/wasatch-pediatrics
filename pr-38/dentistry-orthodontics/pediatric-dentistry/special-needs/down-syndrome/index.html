Special Needs Care

Down Syndrome

Children with Down syndrome benefit from a warm, patient dental team that knows their unique needs. We provide gentle, individualized care every visit.

Why Regular Dental Care Matters

Children with Down syndrome can be more prone to gum issues, delayed or unusual tooth eruption, and crowding. Regular checkups and cleanings let us watch development closely and keep their smile healthy, and our orthodontic care is right here if it's ever needed.

When Sedation Can Help

If your child feels anxious or needs extensive treatment, safe sedation options keep care comfortable. We'll choose the gentlest approach that meets their needs.

We'd love to get to know your child, and to make each visit a little easier than the last.

Common questions

How often should my child be seen?

Most children do best with a checkup and cleaning every six months, though we may suggest more frequent visits to monitor gum health and tooth development.

Do you offer orthodontics too?

Yes. Because we provide dentistry and orthodontics under one roof, we can monitor your child's bite and bring in our orthodontist when treatment makes sense.
