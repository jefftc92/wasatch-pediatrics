What to Do After Hours

Call (801) 676-3700 and follow the prompts for after-hours phone support. We can help you calm the pain, manage a dental injury, and decide whether your child needs to be seen right away or first thing when we reopen.

Go to the ER first for:

Uncontrolled bleeding, a possible broken jaw, or facial swelling that affects breathing or swallowing. Call 911 or head to the nearest emergency room, then call us for the dental follow-up.

Easing Pain Until Your Visit

A comfortable night is very doable with a few basics.

1

Children's pain reliever

An age-appropriate dose takes the edge off.

2

Cold compress

On the outside of the cheek for pain or swelling.

3

Warm saltwater rinse

Calms sore gums and cleans the area gently.

4

Save any pieces

A knocked-out tooth or fragment goes in milk and comes with you.

Why Calling Beats Waiting and Worrying

Most nighttime dental scares have a clear answer: what helps now, and when to be seen. Getting that answer from us in five minutes beats a sleepless night of guessing, and if the situation is truly urgent, we'd much rather know tonight. For visual concerns, a virtual visit may also be an option.

Who This Is For

Any family with a child's dental problem outside office hours, current patients and new ones alike. Toothaches, injuries, swelling, braces trouble: call, and we'll guide you through the night and get your child cared for as soon as possible with a same-day appointment.

A hurting kid at 9pm is stressful. A five-minute call gets you a plan, and a much better night for everyone.

Common questions

Can I reach someone outside office hours?

Yes. Call (801) 676-3700 and follow the prompts for after-hours phone support so we can guide you and arrange care as soon as possible.

Should I go to the ER for a dental emergency?

Go to the ER for uncontrolled bleeding, trouble breathing or swallowing, or a suspected broken jaw. For tooth and gum problems, call us and we'll see your child quickly.

Will my child be seen the next morning?

We reserve time for emergencies, so a child who called overnight is a priority when we open. We'll set it up during your call.
