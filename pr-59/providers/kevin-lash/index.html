Background

Dr. Kevin Lash attended medical school at the University of Utah and completed his pediatrics residency at the University of Minnesota in Minneapolis/St. Paul. He is board-certified in Pediatrics and has been practicing at our Southpoint location since 1995.  Dr. Lash enjoys working together with children and their families.  His clinical interests include mental health and ADHD concerns.

He is married and has two sons.  His hobbies include soccer, music, cooking, gardening and enjoying the outdoors. He speaks Spanish and has volunteered in various communities in Latin America.
