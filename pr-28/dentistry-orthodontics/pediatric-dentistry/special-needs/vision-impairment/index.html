A Tell-and-Touch Approach

We describe each step in clear, friendly language before it happens, and let your child feel a tool or texture when it helps. Narrating the visit removes surprises and helps your child stay calm and in control.

How We Narrate the Visit

Words and touch do the work that sight usually would.

1

Describe first

We explain each step in friendly language before it happens.

2

Let them feel

Your child can touch a tool or texture when it helps them understand.

3

A steady voice

A calm, reassuring tone guides your child through the whole visit.

4

A familiar routine

We keep the sequence the same each time, so it becomes predictable.

Why Narration Builds Trust

Consistency builds confidence. When your child always knows what's coming, described in advance and kept the same from visit to visit, the dental chair becomes a place they can predict and control. That sense of control is what lets them relax and lets us provide gentle, thorough care.

When Sedation Can Help

If treatment is hard to tolerate, safe sedation options keep your child comfortable. We'll recommend the lightest, safest approach for their needs.

Your child will always know what comes next. We'll narrate every step, and build trust one visit at a time.

Common questions

How do you help my child feel comfortable?

We narrate every step before it happens and let your child touch tools when it helps, so nothing is a surprise. A familiar routine each visit builds trust.

What should I tell you beforehand?

Share how your child likes to communicate, what reassures them, and any sensitivities, so we can tailor the visit to them.
