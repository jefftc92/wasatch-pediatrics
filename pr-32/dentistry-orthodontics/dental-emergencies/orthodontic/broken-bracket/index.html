Orthodontic Emergency

Broken Bracket

A bracket that comes loose or pops off is one of the most common braces mishaps, and it's usually not urgent. Here's how to stay comfortable until we repair it.

All Dental Emergencies

What to Do Right Now

A few easy steps and you're covered until the repair.

1

Leave it if attached

If the bracket is still on the wire, leave it in place and don't wiggle it.

2

Wax any sharp spot

A dab of orthodontic wax stops rubbing and irritation instantly.

3

Save it if it's off

If the bracket came off completely, keep it and bring it with you.

4

Call us

Phone (801) 676-3700 and we'll schedule a quick repair.

Is It an Emergency?

Usually not. A loose bracket can wait for a regular repair appointment unless it's causing pain or a poking wire you can't manage. Call us and we'll tell you whether to come in right away or at the next opening.

When to come in sooner

Real pain you can't manage with wax, a wire badly out of place, or a swallowed piece. Call and we'll fit you in.

How We Fix It

We simply re-bond the bracket and replace the wire or elastic if needed. Repair visits are quick, and we'll also make sure the issue isn't slowing your treatment, adjusting the plan if necessary so your braces stay on schedule.

Why Brackets Come Loose

Most broken brackets trace back to hard, sticky, or chewy foods, think ice, hard candy, caramel, and gum, or an accidental knock during sports. Sticking to the braces-friendly food list and wearing a mouthguard for sports prevents most repeat visits.

A popped bracket is a detour, not a setback. Wax it, call us, and we'll have it fixed in one quick visit.

Common questions

Will a broken bracket delay my treatment?

A single loose bracket repaired promptly usually has little effect. Letting it go for a long time can slow progress, so call us to schedule a quick fix.

My bracket is rubbing my cheek. What can I do?

Cover the bracket or wire with orthodontic wax to stop the irritation, and rinse with warm saltwater if your cheek is sore. Then call us for a repair.

What if my child swallowed the bracket?

A swallowed bracket usually passes without trouble, but call us, and seek medical care if there's any coughing or difficulty breathing.
