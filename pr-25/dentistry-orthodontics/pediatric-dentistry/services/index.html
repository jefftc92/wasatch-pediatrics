Prevention first, gentle treatment when needed

Most of what we do is designed to keep your child out of the dental chair for anything but a cleaning. Regular cleanings, fluoride, and sealants stop the vast majority of cavities before they start, and we coach families on brushing, flossing, and diet at every visit.

When a tooth does need care, we treat it gently, catching decay early enough that Curodont can sometimes rebuild enamel without a drill, or placing a natural-looking tooth-colored filling.

How we keep every visit gentle

Every treatment is explained in plain language and paced for your child, so visits stay calm and positive from the very first one. We show the friendly tools, move at their pace, and celebrate the small wins.

For children who feel anxious or need more involved care, safe sedation options and patient, individualized special needs care make sure every child can get the treatment they need.

Whatever your child's smile needs, we'll care for it gently, and keep prevention at the heart of every visit.

Common questions

What dental services do you offer for kids?

Everything in one place: cleanings, fluoride, and sealants for prevention, plus tooth-colored fillings, crowns, pulp therapy, space maintainers, extractions, and modern Curodont cavity care. Braces and clear aligners are here too through our orthodontist.

How often should my child come in?

Most children do best with a checkup and cleaning every six months, starting by age one. We may suggest more frequent visits for kids who are prone to cavities.

Do you offer sedation for treatment?

Yes. From laughing gas to sleep dentistry, safe sedation options keep anxious kids and bigger treatments calm and comfortable. We always recommend the lightest option that fits.

Need to be seen?

Call your office first — most pediatric dentistry are seen the same day.
