Special Needs Dentistry

Every child deserves gentle, judgment-free dental care. Our sensory-friendly team takes extra time, adapts to your child, and partners with you to make each visit a success.

Care built around your child

Children with special healthcare needs thrive when the dental visit works for them. We slow down, keep things predictable, communicate in the way your child understands best, and create a calm, sensory-aware environment in our brand-new Southpoint office.

You know your child best, so we listen first. Tell us what helps and what to avoid, and we'll build the visit around it, with safe sedation options available whenever they make care more comfortable.

How we partner with you

The best visits start before your child ever sits in the chair. We ask what helps and what to avoid, plan the pace and the environment around your child, and welcome a short get-to-know-us visit first so the real appointment feels familiar.

Whether your child needs extra time, a quieter room, visual cues, or gentle sedation, we'll shape the day around them, and celebrate every bit of progress along the way.

Every child deserves a dentist who meets them where they are. Tell us about yours, and we'll plan a visit that works.

Common questions

Can we visit the office before the first appointment?

Yes. A short, low-pressure visit to meet the team and see the space can make the real appointment far easier. Just ask when you book.

What should I share about my child beforehand?

Tell us how your child communicates best, what soothes them, any triggers to avoid, and the routines that work at home. Your insight shapes the whole visit.

Do you offer sedation for special needs care?

Yes. When staying calm and still is too much, safe sedation options, including general anesthesia, let us complete care comfortably. We always recommend the lightest option that fits.

Need to be seen?

Call your office first — most pediatric dentistry are seen the same day.
