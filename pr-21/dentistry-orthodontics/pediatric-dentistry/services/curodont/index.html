What Curodont Is

Curodont is a modern treatment for cavities caught in their earliest stage. Applied to the affected spot, it works with the tooth to rebuild weakened enamel from within, often stopping a small cavity before it grows into one that needs a filling.

How It's Applied

Fast, painless, and easy, it fits right into a regular checkup.

1

Clean the spot

We gently clean the early cavity so it's ready to treat.

2

Apply Curodont

We apply it directly to the affected spot in just a couple of minutes.

3

Rebuilds enamel

It works with the tooth to strengthen the weakened area from within.

4

On your way

No drilling, no numbing, and no recovery, so your child is right back to their day.

Why It's a Gentle Option

Because there's no drilling and no numbing, Curodont is one of the gentlest ways to treat early decay, making it especially good for young children and kids who feel anxious at the dentist. It treats the problem in the least invasive way possible.

When & Who It's For

Curodont works best on cavities found in their earliest stage, which is exactly why regular cleanings and checkups matter so much. It's ideal for children with early, just-forming cavities, and a welcome option for kids who are nervous about traditional treatment or who have special healthcare needs. More advanced decay may still need a filling or crown.

When we catch a cavity early enough, we can often stop it with no drill and no needles, exactly the kind of gentle care kids deserve.

Common questions

What is Curodont?

Curodont is a modern treatment that rebuilds enamel and can stop an early cavity before it needs a filling. It is painless and requires no drilling.

Is Curodont right for every cavity?

It works best on cavities caught in their earliest stage. More advanced decay may still need a filling or crown, which we'll discuss with you.

Does Curodont hurt?

No. There is no drilling and no needles. We simply apply it to the tooth, making it a great option for young or anxious children.
