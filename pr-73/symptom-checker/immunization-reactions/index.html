Reactions After Immunizations

A vaccine reaction is a symptom that occurs as the immune system responds to a vaccination. Mild soreness, redness, swelling, fussiness or fever can occur after some vaccines.

Most reactions come from the expected immune response or irritation at the injection site. Allergic reactions and other serious vaccine reactions are much less common. Symptoms that happen after vaccination can also be unrelated to the vaccine.

When to see a doctor

Contact your pediatrician or another healthcare professional if:

  • Fever or other symptoms are severe or last longer than expected.
  • Redness or swelling at the injection site keeps spreading or becomes increasingly painful.
  • Your child cries inconsolably for a prolonged period or seems very ill.
  • You are worried about a reaction after vaccination.

Call 911 or get emergency care if:

  • Your child has trouble breathing.
  • Your child develops swelling of the tongue, throat or face with other allergic symptoms.
  • Your child collapses or has another sign of a severe allergic reaction.
