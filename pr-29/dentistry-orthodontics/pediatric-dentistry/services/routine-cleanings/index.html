What a Cleaning Includes

During a routine cleaning we gently remove plaque and tartar, polish your child's teeth, and check every tooth for early signs of decay. When it helps, we finish with a fluoride treatment to strengthen enamel.

We explain each step in kid-friendly language and move at a pace that keeps your child relaxed, turning a cleaning into a positive experience.

Why Regular Cleanings Matter

Cleanings remove the buildup a toothbrush can't reach, protecting your child against cavities and gum irritation, and they let us spot tiny problems while they're still easy and affordable to treat. Regular visits also keep your child's dental home active, so we already know their history if a question or emergency ever comes up.

When & Who Cleanings Are For

Most children do best with a cleaning and checkup every six months, starting around their first birthday or within six months of their first tooth. They're for every child, from toddlers to teens, and we take extra time with kids who feel nervous or who have special healthcare needs. Children who are more prone to cavities may benefit from more frequent visits.

A calm cleaning your child won't dread, and a healthy smile that only gets easier to keep.

Common questions

How often should my child get their teeth cleaned?

Most children do best with a professional cleaning every six months. Some kids who are prone to cavities may benefit from more frequent visits, which we'll discuss with you.

Does a dental cleaning hurt?

No. Cleanings are gentle and comfortable. Your child may feel a little pressure or tickling during polishing, but the process is painless and we go at their pace.

At what age should cleanings start?

Around age one, or within six months of the first tooth. Starting early keeps baby teeth healthy and helps your child get comfortable with the dentist.

What if my child is nervous about a cleaning?

We're used to nervous first-timers. We explain everything in simple terms, keep things calm, and offer extra support when needed.
