When a Virtual Visit Helps

Teledentistry is a great first step for a toothache, a small chip, a lost filling, a poking braces wire, or general questions. We can look at the problem over video, reassure you, and decide together whether your child needs to be seen in person.

Why Start Virtual?

Half the battle in a dental scare is knowing whether it's urgent. A video visit answers that in minutes, without loading a hurting child into the car, and often ends with simple reassurance and home care. When it is urgent, you arrive with the diagnosis already started.

When You Should Come In Instead

Some emergencies need hands-on care from the start, such as a knocked-out tooth, facial swelling, or heavy bleeding. When that's the case, skip the screen: call us and we'll get your child into the office, or to the ER, as quickly as possible.

Sometimes the best first appointment is the one you don't have to drive to. We're a video call away.

Common questions

Can a virtual visit fix the problem?

Teledentistry is for guidance and triage. We can ease your worry, recommend home care, and tell you whether your child needs to come in. Hands-on treatment still happens in the office.

How do I start a teledentistry visit?

Call (801) 676-3700 and we'll set up a secure video visit and walk you through the simple steps.

Is a virtual visit good enough for a toothache?

It's a great first step. We can often spot the likely cause on video, guide home care, and schedule in-person treatment at the right urgency.
