Emergency Treatment

Emergency Fillings

When a cavity becomes painful, a same-day filling stops the ache and protects the tooth. One visit, one gentle repair, and your child is back to their day.

All Dental Emergencies

When You Need an Emergency Filling

If your child has a painful tooth, sensitivity that won't quit, or a visible hole from a cavity, a prompt filling relieves the discomfort and stops decay from spreading. We'll see your child quickly and treat the source of the cavity pain.

Same-day relief

Numb, clean, and fill, often in a single visit the day you call.

Natural-looking repair

A tooth-colored filling that blends right into the smile.

Gentle and calm

Thorough numbing and sedation options for anxious kids.

What Happens at the Visit

Quick and calmer than you'd expect.

1

Numb gently

We numb the tooth so there's nothing sharp to feel.

2

Clean out the decay

Only the decayed part is removed, keeping healthy tooth.

3

Fill and bond

A natural-looking tooth-colored filling seals the tooth.

4

Check and go

We match the bite and your child is on their way.

Why Treating It Today Matters

A hurting cavity never heals on its own. Treated today, it's a simple filling. Left alone, decay keeps advancing toward the nerve, where the same tooth may need pulp therapy, a crown, or removal. Prompt treatment keeps both the pain and the fix small.

If the Cavity Is Too Deep

When decay has reached the nerve, a filling alone won't solve the pain. In that case we may recommend pulp therapy and a crown. We'll always choose the gentlest treatment that lasts.

A painful cavity has a same-day ending. Call us, and your child sleeps comfortably tonight.

Common questions

Can a filling be done the same day?

Often, yes. If a painful cavity is caught in time, we can usually numb, clean, and fill the tooth in one visit to bring quick relief.

Will my child be in pain during the filling?

No. We numb the tooth first and keep the visit comfortable, with sedation available for anxious children.

What if the pain is severe or wakes my child at night?

That can mean the decay is deep. Call us right away; we'll see your child quickly and treat whatever the tooth needs.
