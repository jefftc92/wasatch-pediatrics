Welcome!

As one of the founding offices of Wasatch Pediatrics, our pediatric clinic has been serving generations of families for over 30 years.  Our outstanding team of pediatric physicians and staff is dedicated to the care of your children, and we appreciate the trust you put in us and our care.

We pride ourselves in offering a complete medical home to our patients and their families.  Our office is a place where you can come to ask questions, receive exceptional pediatric primary care, and have important conversations about your child’s whole health with the very best pediatricians in Salt Lake City.
