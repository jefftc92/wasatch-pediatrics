Welcome to our office!

We understand how important your child’s health and well-being are to you. Our goal is to provide the highest quality medical care and helping to meet the health needs of your family. We love seeing our patients grow and thrive, and patients from all backgrounds are welcome.

Our office has had the privilege of providing pediatric primary care to families in the Salt Lake Valley for over 30 years.  With our team of amazing pediatric providers and staff, we pride ourselves in serving as the medical home for all of our patients from newborns to teenagers.  We are able to accept almost all health insurance plans offered in Utah, including SelectHealth.

In Spring 2025, we moved into a brand-new office space and can’t wait for you to see it!

*If you were previously a Sunnyside Pediatrics patient and are looking for medical records, you’ve come to the right place.  Call us to request your records, or in the meantime, stop by for a visit or checkup!
