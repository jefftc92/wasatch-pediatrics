Still concerned?  Our experts are just a call away to help make it ALL BETTER!  Contact us to schedule an appointment or reach our triage line after hours.

What to do right now

The Symptom Checker below is for working out whether a symptom needs a doctor. If you already know it does, here is where each kind of problem goes.

  • An emergency — trouble breathing, unresponsiveness, a seizure, or a serious injury. Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room. Do not wait for a call back.
  • You want to talk to someone now — we run a 24/7 on-call nurse and physician line. Call your office’s main number at any hour and you will reach it.
  • Your child needs to be seen today — our offices hold same-day appointments for illness and injury.
  • It is an evening, a weekend or a holiday — most of our offices run After Hours Care. These are same-day urgent visits by appointment; there are no walk-ins, and hours differ by office.
  • You are not sure — start with the Symptom Checker below, then call us if it tells you to or if you are still uneasy.

If you are worried, call. We would rather answer a question that turns out to be nothing than have you sit up all night deciding whether it was.

What we handle ourselves

Same-day appointments at our offices cover illness and injury, from sore throats and rashes to wound treatment and more serious injuries such as concussions and the initial care of fractures. Most of what sends a family looking at a symptom checker at nine at night is something we can see the next morning.

What we cannot do is treat an emergency. Anything involving breathing, consciousness, a seizure or a major injury belongs in an emergency room, and calling 911 is faster than calling us.

Common questions

Who answers if I call at night?

A nurse or physician. We run an on-call line 24 hours a day, and it is reached through your office’s ordinary main number rather than a separate one to remember.

Do I need an appointment for After Hours Care?

Yes. After Hours visits are for same-day urgent concerns and are by appointment only — there are no walk-ins. Call first.

Is After Hours Care at every office?

At most of them, and the hours differ from one office to the next. Your office’s own page lists its hours.

Should I use the Symptom Checker or just call?

Either. The Symptom Checker is good for deciding whether something needs a doctor at all. If it says to be seen, or if you are worried regardless of what it says, call — a symptom checker cannot hear the cough or ask the second question.

My child is sick and we are not patients yet. Can we still be seen?

Call the office nearest you and ask. It is also worth reading about becoming a patient, though that does not have to happen first.
