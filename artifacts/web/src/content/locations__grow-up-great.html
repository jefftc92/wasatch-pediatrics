Welcome to our office!

Our roots lie heavy in our beginnings as Grow Up Great Pediatrics, but since joining Wasatch Pediatrics in 2000, we’ve never stopped providing that “cradle to college” care that your family can trust. Thank you for choosing us to care for your children.

It’s our honor to provide exceptional pediatric primary care to families in Bountiful and the neighboring communities, and we look forward to doing so for many more years to come. Our pediatric clinic provides a wide range of services to aid in your child’s overall health and well-being.
