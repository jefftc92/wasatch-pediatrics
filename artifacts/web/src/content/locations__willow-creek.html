Welcome to Willow Creek

As one of the founding offices of Wasatch Pediatrics over 30 years ago, our clinic has a long history of caring for generations of families across the Salt Lake Valley. We pride ourselves on striving to meet the unique needs of each patient and providing excellent pediatric primary care that families can trust when they need it.

We love caring for children of all ages, abilities, and backgrounds, and believe the best care is obtained by working in partnership with patients and families. Our goal is to listen to concerns and be a source of support and expert guidance for your child’s whole health, including medical, physical, and mental health aspects.

Our team of amazing pediatric doctors, providers, and staff look forward to welcoming you to the Willow Creek family and serving as your child’s complete medical home.
