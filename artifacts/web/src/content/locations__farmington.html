Welcome!

At Wasatch Pediatrics Farmington, we love caring for kids and know your child’s well-being is of utmost importance to you.  That’s why we strive to provide exceptional pediatric primary care in a timely, compassionate, and child-friendly way.  We want your experience with us to be great.

Our amazing team of pediatric physicians and providers cares for a wide-range of pediatric needs for children of all ages and backgrounds.  In addition to treating common illnesses and childhood conditions, we pride ourselves on preventive care you can trust with annual check-ups to keep kids healthy, catch problems early, and guide parenting solutions.
