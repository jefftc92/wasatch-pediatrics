Choosing a Pediatrician

Choosing the right pediatrician for your child is one of the most important decisions you will make. At Wasatch Pediatrics, you can feel confident and comfortable that you have made the right decision.

We’ve got you covered with your choice of over 50 board-certified pediatricians in 8 different office locations in Davis, Salt Lake, and Summit counties. Our pediatricians have unique patient care styles, varied clinical interests, and different backgrounds, allowing you to choose the doctor that is the best fit for your family.

To help ease the stress of getting started with a new pediatrician, we offer free meet-and-greet appointments for families who are expecting a new child or families with children of any age who are new to our practice or area.

Contact us to schedule a visit or learn more about our providers.

Care When You Need It

Our offices conveniently offer same-day appointments for illnesses and injuries. Check our locations pages for office-specific hours along with guidance on receiving care after-hours and on weekends.

If your primary pediatrician is not available, we’ll schedule an appointment with one of their skilled partners. You can expect compassionate care with the consistency of the same support team, electronic health record, and warm and welcoming environment.

Preparing for Your Visit

What to Bring

Please bring any available copies of your child’s medical and immunization records. We will also ask to see your health insurance card and photo identification. Please plan to arrive early for your first visit to allow time to finish your registration. It is helpful if you complete your registration forms before arriving.

You can expect to receive a reminder call, email, or text a few days before your appointment.

 

What to Expect

Well Child Checkups

We’ll take a team approach to your child’s whole health, including tracking growth and development, addressing behavioral health, performing screenings, providing guidance on prevention & safety, and discussing any of your concerns.

Check out this video to learn more.

Childhood Immunizations

Protecting your child against serious illness is a critical piece of their overall wellness, and immunizations play a key role. If your child is due for vaccines, we’ll discuss recommendations & help answer any of your questions.

Behavioral Health Visits

Therapy and medication management visits are conducted by our in-house team of multidisciplinary professionals, including therapists, pediatricians, and mental health specialists. Our integrated approach helps treat both the body and mind for optimal whole health.

Sick Visits

Whether your child is ill, injured, or you just need reassurance, we’re here with expert care, advice, and resources.
