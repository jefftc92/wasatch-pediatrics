Welcome to Wasatch Pediatrics – Draper!

We have a long tradition of building trusted, lifelong relationships with our patients and their families, generation after generation. And we’re always changing and growing—just like your kids! That includes welcoming new pediatricians to join our team of world-class caregivers as we say farewell to some of our favorite retiring physicians.

A few names and faces might feel new, but one thing that will never change is our commitment to your child’s care. We continue to strive to provide compassionate care that is focused on the health and wellness of your child and family. Thank you for choosing us to care for your kids!
