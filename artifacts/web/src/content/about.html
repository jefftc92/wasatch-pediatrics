About Us

At Wasatch Pediatrics, we believe there’s nothing better than a healthy, happy child. With an emphasis on individualized, family-centered care and a personal, friendly approach, your child’s wellness is our top priority. We are eager to partner with you in caring for your kids, as we strive to provide a listening ear, a helping hand, and superior healthcare.

Whether providing care for your child or helping you navigate that care—our goal is to make it ALL BETTER. That’s our promise, and it’s at the heart of everything we do.

Our Promises

Healthcare that Heals

When your child is sick, hurt, or struggling, getting better is the top priority. Healing is at the heart of our work and you can count on our expert pediatricians, nurses and staff to provide results-driven healthcare.

Priority on Prevention

By instilling good eating, exercise, and mental health habits in patients at a young age, we work to prevent illness, ensure whole health, and pave the road to a better quality of life.

An Ear and an Answer

We encourage reliable, two-way communication. Our physicians, nurses, and staff provide a listening ear first and a satisfactory answer second.

A Place for Kids

We strive to create a relaxing, welcoming, and inviting environment that puts you and your kids at ease.
