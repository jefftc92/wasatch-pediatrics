Welcome to Southpoint!

We pride ourselves on caring for families and helping them navigate all the ups and downs of raising children and keeping them healthy. It can be stressful, and we’re here to help you every step of the way.

Our office has an amazing team of the very best pediatricians in West Jordan as well as nurse practitioners, child psychologists, a registered dietitian, and patient advocates to care for all of your child’s needs. We believe that effective care means partnering with you as a team. We are deeply honored to have the chance to help your children grow strong and healthy – thank you for trusting us to be part of your family.

We welcome patients from all backgrounds and pride ourselves on being a complete medical home for your child.
