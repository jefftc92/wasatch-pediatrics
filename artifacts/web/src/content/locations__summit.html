Welcome to Summit!

We love kids, and our primary goal is to have a positive impact in children’s lives by providing exceptional and compassionate pediatric primary care.  We’re proud to be an integral part of our community serving patients throughout Summit County, Wasatch County, and beyond.  Thank you for entrusting us to care for your children.

We are committed to providing the highest quality service, including creating a comfortable and safe office setting where children and parents can feel at ease.  Our dedicated team of physicians, providers, & staff values setting aside time to equip parents with the tools and resources necessary for the development of healthy children.  We pride ourselves on being there for you every step of the way from birth to adolescence.
