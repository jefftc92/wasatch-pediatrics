ADHD

Sitting still in the dental chair can be a real challenge. We keep visits short, positive, and engaging so children with ADHD can succeed.

Short and positive

Efficient, upbeat visits broken into manageable, easy steps.

Engaging and clear

A friendly pace and clear direction that help your child stay with us.

Sedation if it helps

Safe options for longer treatment when staying still is too much.

Visits Designed to Hold Attention

We keep appointments efficient and upbeat, break treatment into manageable steps, and give clear, encouraging direction. A predictable routine and a friendly pace help your child stay comfortable and cooperative.

How We Keep Visits Engaging

Short, upbeat, and predictable is the winning combination. Here's how we do it.

  1. Keep it short Efficient visits broken into small, manageable steps.
  2. Clear direction Simple, encouraging cues that are easy to follow.
  3. A friendly pace Upbeat and steady, so your child stays comfortable and engaged.
  4. The right timing Many families do best with a morning slot; we'll pick a time that suits your child.

Why Consistency Matters

For kids with ADHD, familiar is easy. Regular checkups and cleanings keep visits short and predictable, and simple at-home tips that fit a busy routine help brushing actually stick. The more routine it all feels, the smoother each visit goes.

When Sedation Can Help

If staying still for longer treatment is too difficult, safe sedation options can help your child relax so we can finish their care comfortably.

Common questions

What if my child cannot sit still?

We keep visits short and engaging, and we're patient. For longer treatment, sedation options can help your child relax and stay comfortable.

Does the timing of the appointment matter?

It can. Many families find a morning visit works best. Tell us what time of day your child tends to do well and we'll try to accommodate it.

Need to be seen?

Call your office first — most pediatric dentistry are seen the same day.
