Same-Day Appointments

Dental problems don't wait, so neither do we. Call us and we'll do everything we can to see your child the same day, with after-hours support when the office is closed.

Time held for emergencies

We reserve room in the schedule every day for urgent visits.

Guidance the moment you call

We'll give you first-aid steps for your child's exact situation.

After-hours support

Phone guidance and teledentistry when the office is closed.

What Counts as a Dental Emergency?

Call us right away for a severe toothache, a knocked-out or broken tooth, facial swelling, bleeding that won't stop, or a dental injury.

When in doubt, call. We'd rather hear from you and help you decide than have you wait and worry.

How Same-Day Care Works

From your call to relief, here's the path.

  1. Call as early as you can Phone (801) 676-3700 and tell us what happened.
  2. First aid over the phone We'll give you specific steps for your child's situation while you head in.
  3. Seen the same day We reserve time for urgent needs, so your child gets in quickly.
  4. Comfort, then treatment We ease the pain first, then treat the cause gently.

Why Calling First Matters

The right first aid in the first hour can change the outcome, especially for a knocked-out permanent tooth, which has the best chance of being saved within 30 to 60 minutes. Calling first also lets us prepare for your child's exact situation, so treatment starts the moment you arrive instead of after a wait.

Who This Is For, and After Hours

Any child (or parent) with an urgent dental problem is welcome, whether you're a current patient or brand new to us. If it's after hours, our phone support can guide you and, when helpful, set up a teledentistry visit, so you're never left without help.

Head to the ER first if there's:

Trouble breathing or swallowing, a possible broken jaw, or bleeding that won't stop. Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room, then call us for the dental follow-up.

Call us and take a breath. We'll get your child seen, comfortable, and smiling again fast.

Common questions

Can you really see my child today?

We reserve time each day for emergencies and will do everything we can to see your child the same day. Call (801) 676-3700 as early as possible.

What if it's after hours?

We offer after-hours phone support to guide you, and teledentistry visits when appropriate, so you are never left without help.

Do you take new patients for emergencies?

Yes. If your child is in pain, call us, whether or not you've been in before. We'll take care of them first and handle the paperwork after.

Need to be seen?

Call your office first — most dental emergencies are seen the same day.
