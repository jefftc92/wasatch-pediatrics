Retainers

Finishing braces or aligners is a big milestone, and a retainer is what keeps that hard-won smile in place. Custom-fit retainers, clear guidance, and fast replacements when life happens.

Protects your results

It holds teeth steady while bone and tissue settle around them.

Fit to your lifestyle

Clear, wire, or fixed behind the teeth, we'll match the right type to you.

Fast replacements

Lost or broken? We'll make a new one before teeth start to shift.

Why You Need a Retainer

After braces or clear aligners, teeth naturally want to drift back toward their old positions. A retainer holds them steady while the surrounding bone and tissue settle, protecting the time and investment you put into a straight smile.

What Types of Retainers Are There?

There are removable clear retainers that look much like aligner trays, traditional wire-and-acrylic retainers, and fixed retainers bonded discreetly behind the teeth. Each has its strengths, and Dr. Wendy Chu will recommend the type that best fits your smile and lifestyle.

How Retainer Wear Works

A simple routine that protects your smile for life.

  1. Full time at first Right after treatment, you'll wear your retainer most of the day and night.
  2. Then nights only Once teeth settle, most patients shift to wearing it while they sleep.
  3. Keep it clean A quick rinse and gentle cleaning as directed keeps it fresh.
  4. For the long haul Wearing it as directed is the single best way to keep your results for life.

Retainer lost or not fitting?

Contact us soon. Teeth can start shifting quickly, and a prompt replacement keeps your smile on track.

Who Needs One, and When

Everyone who finishes orthodontic treatment needs a retainer, whether that treatment was braces or aligners, and whether it ended last month or years ago. If your old retainer no longer fits, or your teeth have drifted since treatment elsewhere, we're happy to help you get back on track.

You earned that smile. A well-worn retainer is the easiest promise you can make to keep it.

Common questions

What happens if I stop wearing my retainer?

Teeth can gradually shift back out of alignment. If your retainer no longer fits or you have lost it, contact us soon so we can help before movement happens.

Can you replace a lost retainer?

Yes. We can make a new retainer to keep your smile on track. Reach out as soon as you notice it is lost or damaged.

How do I clean my retainer?

Rinse it after wearing and clean it gently as directed. We'll show you exactly how to care for your specific type of retainer.

Need to be seen?

Call your office first — most orthodontics are seen the same day.
