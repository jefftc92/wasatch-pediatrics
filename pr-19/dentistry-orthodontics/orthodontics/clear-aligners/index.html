Clear Aligners

Clear aligners straighten teeth with a series of nearly invisible, removable trays. No wires, no brackets, and no food restrictions, just steady progress most people never notice.

Nearly invisible

See-through trays that most people never notice you're wearing.

Removable freedom

Take them out to eat, brush, and floss, with no food restrictions.

Orthodontist-guided

Every plan is designed and tracked by board-certified Dr. Wendy Chu.

What Are Clear Aligners?

Clear aligners are a series of custom, see-through trays that gently shift teeth into place. You wear each set for about two weeks, then move to the next.

Because they're removable and nearly invisible, many teens and adults prefer them to traditional braces, especially when a discreet look matters.

How Aligner Treatment Works

Simple, predictable, and easy to live with. Here's the rhythm.

  1. Scan & plan Dr. Chu maps your smile and designs the full series of custom trays.
  2. Wear each set About two weeks per set, worn 20 to 22 hours a day.
  3. Quick check-ins Occasional visits track progress and hand off your next trays.
  4. Finish & retain A retainer keeps your new smile right where it should be.

Consistency is the secret

Aligners only work while they're on your teeth. Wearing them 20 to 22 hours a day keeps treatment on schedule.

Why Choose Aligners?

Beyond the nearly invisible look, aligners let you eat whatever you like and keep brushing and flossing exactly as you always have, which makes healthy teeth and gums easier during treatment. Smooth plastic also means no brackets or wires to irritate cheeks or manage after adjustments.

Who They're For, and When Braces Fit Better

Aligners work well for many common issues like crowding, spacing, and mild to moderate bite problems, and they're a great fit for motivated teens and adults who will wear them consistently. For younger children or more complex corrections, braces may be the more dependable path. Dr. Chu will evaluate your smile and tell you honestly which option gets the best result.

A straighter smile that fits quietly into your life, and an honest recommendation about whether it's right for yours.

Common questions

How many hours a day do I wear aligners?

For best results, aligners are typically worn around 20 to 22 hours a day, removing them only to eat, drink anything but water, and clean your teeth.

Are clear aligners good for kids?

They work best for motivated teens and adults who will wear them consistently. For younger children, we'll recommend the most effective option.

Can I still eat my favorite foods?

Yes. Because aligners are removable, there are no food restrictions. Just take them out to eat and brush before putting them back in.

How much do clear aligners cost?

Cost depends on how much movement is needed. We offer orthodontic financing and will go over your options up front at a complimentary consultation.
