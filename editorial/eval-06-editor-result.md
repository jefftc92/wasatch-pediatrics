## Critical

1. **croup, a collision created by our own escalation.** "If you can still hear
   stridor while your child is calm and resting, call 911" and "A baby under a
   year old with any stridor at all needs an emergency room now." An infant under
   one with stridor at rest satisfies both, and they give different instructions.
   The later sentence is more specific by age, so a parent reads it as
   overriding. The AAP does not create this collision. Rule 19a now covers it.
2. **diaper-rash, two uniqueness claims in consecutive sentences.** "The
   exception is a rash around the anus during diarrhea" and then "A yeast rash is
   the one that needs a cream." If the anal rash is the exception, a yeast rash
   cannot be the one. The reader must work out that these are two different
   exceptions to two different generalizations.

## Major

3. **diaper-rash, the paragraph loops.** The three-day threshold and its yeast
   conclusion are stated at the start, interrupted by the ointment digression,
   then restated at the end as if new. "Yeast does not clear with cleaning alone"
   and "A yeast rash is the one that needs a cream" are the same point twice.
4. **fever, "has stopped moving".** The AAP sign is "Not moving or too weak to
   stand." "Has stopped moving" converts a state into a change the parent must
   have witnessed, and a child who has lain limp all afternoon has not stopped
   moving at any observable moment. Rule 15 permits more caution, not less.
5. **fever, the 104 reversal is unexplained.** The copy argues that the number is
   not the thing to watch, then hands the parent a number that overrides the
   child in front of them, with "even if your child seems well" contradicting the
   three questions it just taught. No reason is given, so a parent who believed
   the first sentence has grounds to discount the instruction.
6. **diaper-rash, Lotrimin AF.** Rule 17 grounds. **Overruled.** See rule 18 as
   amended: the test is whether the reader can act, not whether the situation is
   an emergency, and the round 4 reader failed this page for withholding the
   name. This is the third consecutive round in which evaluator A has raised it,
   which is why the rule now settles it in writing.
