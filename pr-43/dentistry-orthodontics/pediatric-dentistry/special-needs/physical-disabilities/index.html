Special Needs Care

Physical Disabilities

A child's physical needs should never get in the way of a healthy smile. Our brand-new, wheelchair-accessible Southpoint office is built to welcome and accommodate every family.

All Pediatric Dentistry

Accessible, Accommodating Dentistry

Our office is easy to navigate, and our team is happy to adapt how and where we provide care. For children who use a wheelchair or have limited mobility, we find the most comfortable, supported position for treatment and take the time to get it right.

Accessible and accommodating

A wheelchair-friendly office and a team happy to adapt how and where we work.

Comfortable positioning

Supportive aids and the right position, taken slowly and at your child's pace.

Sedation if it helps

Safe options when holding a position or staying still is difficult.

How We Keep the Visit Comfortable

Comfort and safety guide every choice. Here's what a visit looks like.

1

An accessible office

Easy to enter and move through, with room to maneuver a wheelchair.

2

Supported positioning

We find the most secure, comfortable position, often right in your child's own chair.

3

A patient pace

We move slowly, communicate each step, and never rush.

4

Extra support

Positioning aids and, when needed, sedation keep care gentle.

Why Comfort and Access Matter

When a child feels physically secure and supported, they can relax, and relaxed children get better, gentler care. Getting the position and the pace right isn't a detail; it's what makes a thorough, comfortable visit possible in the first place.

When Sedation Can Help

When holding a position or staying still for treatment is difficult, safe sedation options can make care far more comfortable. We'll help you choose the gentlest approach for your child.

Your child's physical needs will never get in the way of a healthy smile. We'll make care accessible and comfortable.

Common questions

Is your office wheelchair accessible?

Yes. Our brand-new Southpoint office is easy to access, and our team will help find the most comfortable way to provide your child's care.

Can my child be treated in their wheelchair?

Often, yes. We adapt to what is safest and most comfortable for your child, and we'll discuss the best approach with you beforehand.
